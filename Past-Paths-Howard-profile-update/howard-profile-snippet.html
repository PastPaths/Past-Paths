Narrator

Howard Leader

Howard Leader trained as an actor and early in his career toured widely in theatre productions in the UK and abroad. He spent four years in the cast of the hit comedy show ‘Allo ‘Allo, appearing in television episodes and in the show’s theatre cast in seasons at the Prince of Wales’s Theatre, the London Palladium, and international tours.

His career took on an extra dimension when he became part of Esther Rantzen’s BBC That’s Life! programme, on which he was a weekly presenter for five years. Thereafter, Howard continued to present on television and radio for the BBC and independent broadcasters, developed a journalistic strand as a writer for newspapers and magazines, and grew into writing and directing documentary and news feature material.

Howard says, “I was delighted to be invited to narrate these Past Paths audio productions designed to share the wonderful stories from Britain’s rich history with listeners exploring its fascinating places.”
